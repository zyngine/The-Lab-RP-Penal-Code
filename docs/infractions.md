# Infractions

The lowest tier. Mostly traffic and public-order offences; several carry no jail time at all.

8 charges.

| Code | Charge | Description | Jail | Fine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PC-1.01` | **Speeding** | Driving faster than the posted speed limit on a public roadway. | 0 Months | $500 |
| `PC-1.02` | **Reckless Driving** | Driving dangerously, racing, speeds, traveling >150mph or operating a vehicle recklessly. | 0 Months | $1,000 |
| `PC-1.03` | **Failure to Comply** | Refusing lawful instructions from law enforcement during a stop or investigation. | 5 Months | $1,000 |
| `PC-1.04` | **Disorderly Conduct** | Causing public disturbances, fighting, excessive disruption, or unsafe public behavior. | 5 Months | $1,500 |
| `PC-1.05` | **Trespassing** | Entering or remaining on private or restricted property without permission. | 10 Months | $2,000 |
| `PC-1.06` | **Illegal Parking / Vehicle Abandonment** | Parking illegally or leaving vehicles unattended for unreasonable periods of time. | 0 Months | $1,000 |
| `PC-1.07` | **Failure to Appear** | Failing to appear for scheduled court proceedings without lawful excuse. | 10 Months | $3,000 |
| `PC-1.08` | **Contempt of Court** | Disrupting court proceedings or refusing lawful court orders. | 10 Months | $2,500 |
